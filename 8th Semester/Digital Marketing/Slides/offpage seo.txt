Link Building:

Types of Backlinks:
Do Follow: website A allowing crawler to move from website A to Website B
No Follow: website A doesnpt allow crawler to move from Web A to Web B. (Pinterest, Quora, medium) - More traffic
High Quality Backlink: Linking with the website of same catergory.
Tenchnology --> technology related website

Rule for identifying websites for creating backlink
1. The domain authroity of the other website should be greater than yours
2. The DA should be more than 20
3. Spam score should be less than 5%
